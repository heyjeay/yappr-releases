## Get started

1. **Download** [Yappr.zip](https://github.com/heyjeay/yappr-releases/releases/latest/download/Yappr.zip).
2. **Unzip it** wherever you like: your Desktop, Documents, or a Games folder.
3. **Open `Yappr.exe`.**
4. **Make an account** with your email and a password. You'll join the community server
   straight away, and get to pick your team.

### "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows shows this for apps from small developers it hasn't heard of yet. Click
**More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only have to do this once.

## What you can do

- **Servers and channels**: text and voice channels, organised into categories.
- **Voice chat, video and screen sharing**: hop into a voice channel or call a friend.
- **Friends and DMs**: add people by their tag (like `Jeay#1`), message them, or send
  yourself notes.
- **Teams**: pick one of six teams and join its own server. You can switch once a week.
- **Klips**: a feed of the videos people post.
- **Make it yours**: themes, fonts, sounds for everything, your own profile picture and
  banner, and custom emoji for your servers.
- **Folders**: drag one server onto another to group them in your server list.

## Updates

Yappr keeps itself up to date. When a new version is ready it downloads in the background,
and the next time you open Yappr you're on it. You can check by hand, or turn automatic
updates off, in **Settings → About**.

Every update is checked against Yappr's digital signature before it installs, so only real
Yappr updates ever get in.

## Good to know

- **Works on** Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).
- **Your account lives on the Yappr server**, not on your PC, so you can sign in from any
  computer.
- **Moving Yappr to another folder?** Close it first, move the whole folder, then open it again.
